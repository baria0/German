## Can-do checklist

Tick an item when you have done it **without help**, in a session or in real life. These are the B1 targets for April 2027.

### Phase 1 (A2 solid)
- [ ] I can introduce myself, my studies and my life in Darmstadt for 3 minutes without stopping.
- [ ] I can order, pay and ask questions in a bakery, café or Mensa only in German.
- [ ] I can make an appointment by phone.
- [ ] I can tell what I did yesterday and last weekend (Perfekt).
- [ ] I can give reasons with *weil* and *dass* with the verb at the end.

### Phase 2 (everyday problems)
- [ ] I can describe symptoms to a doctor or at a pharmacy.
- [ ] I can explain a problem in my flat to a landlord or Hausmeister.
- [ ] I can handle a delay or cancellation with Deutsche Bahn.
- [ ] I can return a product or make a polite complaint.
- [ ] I can understand an official letter and know what to do.

### Phase 3 (real conversations)
- [ ] I can give my opinion, justify it and react to another opinion.
- [ ] I can explain what aerospace engineering is and what I do, in simple words.
- [ ] I can hold a 10 minute conversation on a familiar topic without switching to English.
- [ ] I can understand most of a Super Easy German or Easy German street interview with German subtitles.
- [ ] I can follow the main points of *Tagesschau in 100 Sekunden*.

### Phase 4 (B1 exam ready)
- [ ] I can plan something with a partner (Sprechen Teil 1).
- [ ] I can give a 3 minute presentation with pros, cons and my own experience (Sprechen Teil 2).
- [ ] I can write formal and informal emails of 80 to 100 words.
- [ ] I passed a full B1 mock exam.
- [ ] I booked the B1 exam.

### 🇬🇧 English upgrades
- [ ] I use present perfect correctly with *since* and *for*.
- [ ] I can translate German sentences into natural English, not word by word.
- [ ] I actively use 20 new C1 collocations or phrasal verbs from the English corner.
