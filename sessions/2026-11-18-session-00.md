# Session 00 · 2026-11-18 · Setup

**Week:** W0 (setup and baseline) · **Duration:** setup only

## Goal

Build the learning system: method, plan with timeline, resources, mistake log, progress tracking and the tutor protocol for daily sessions.

## What we did

1. Researched resources that help with **speaking**, not just grammar: Easy German, DW Nicos Weg, nachrichtenleicht, Slow German, Language Transfer, Tandem and HelloTalk, TU Darmstadt SPZ, h_da Sprachcafé. Result: [RESOURCES.md](../RESOURCES.md).
2. Decided the method: speaking first, chunks, grammar on demand, mistakes as data, daily input, real life missions. Result: [METHOD.md](../METHOD.md).
3. Decided how to combine German and English: German is the target, English is the working language (explanations, translations, English corner). Translations of texts are folded so the German is tried first.
4. Wrote a 22 week plan to B1 (exam May 2027) and a B2 track to December 2027. Result: [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md).
5. Created the phrase deck with 41 high-frequency conversation chunks: [vocab/chunks.tsv](../vocab/chunks.tsv).

## Homework (before Session 01)

- [ ] Install **Anki** and import `vocab/chunks.tsv` (instructions at the end of RESOURCES.md).
- [ ] Install **Tandem** or **HelloTalk** and set up a profile (offer Turkish and English, look for German).
- [ ] Watch one **Super Easy German** video on the Easy German YouTube channel.
- [ ] Set your phone keyboard dictation to German.
- [ ] **Baseline (CP0):** answer the 5 baseline questions in German (dictated, no dictionary, no preparation). Mistakes are welcome: they are the starting point.

### Baseline questions

1. Wer bist du, und was machst du in Darmstadt?
2. Wie sieht ein normaler Tag bei dir aus?
3. Was hast du letztes Wochenende gemacht?
4. Warum lernst du Deutsch, und was ist für dich am schwierigsten?
5. Was möchtest du in einem Jahr auf Deutsch können?
